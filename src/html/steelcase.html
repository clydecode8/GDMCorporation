STEELCASE

MID VALLEY, KL

For Steelcase at Mid Valley, the client preferred an open-space office concept with private zones for employee comfort and performance. We created a unique style for the office, designing it as a destination and illustrating both a showroom and office in The Garden South Tower, Mid Valley City. The design incorporates a "nature-feel" using timber and indoor plants to enhance the mood.
